Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  UnveilArt, Collingwood, Victoria, Australia

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies our website uses, and how you can control them. It is provided in the interest of transparency consistent with Australian Privacy Principle 1 (APP 1) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to the site owner. Cookies may be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (stored for a set period).

Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels. This policy covers all such technologies used on our Site.

2. Our Approach

UnveilArt does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) that monitor your behaviour across websites. Our use of cookies and similar technologies is minimal and limited to what is necessary for the Site to function.

4. What We Do Not Use

UnveilArt does not use:

  • Google Analytics or any other analytics tracking service
  • Facebook Pixel or any social media tracking pixel
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Cookies that track your behaviour across other websites
  • Any cookies that collect sensitive personal information

5. Australian Privacy Law and Cookies

Under Australian law, cookies that collect personal information (such as IP addresses linked to identifiable individuals) are subject to the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. Our use of third-party services (Google Fonts, Formspree, GitHub Pages) is disclosed in this policy consistent with APP 1 (open and transparent management of personal information) and APP 3 (collection of solicited personal information).

Australia does not currently have a legal requirement to obtain explicit consent for cookies equivalent to the EU's GDPR or the UK's PECR. However, we are committed to transparency and limit our use of cookies to what is genuinely necessary.

6. How to Control Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect how the Site functions.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari (iPhone/iPad): Settings → Safari → Privacy & Security
  • Safari (Mac): Safari → Settings → Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

To opt out of Google's data collection across services, you can visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
