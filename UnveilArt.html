About

Art should be seen.

Not stored in studios, not waiting for the right gallery.

UnveilArt connects Melbourne's emerging artists with the spaces where their work belongs — hotels, restaurants, and venues where people actually live, dine, and remember.

We don't place art once and walk away. We rotate. We refresh. We keep introducing new voices to new walls.

Every wall is an opportunity. Every rotation, a new story.

Based in Melbourne.
Built for the artists and spaces that make this city worth experiencing.
How it works

A practice, not a delivery.

We've designed UnveilArt to be quiet for you and generative for the artists. Three steps that repeat — so the work on your walls keeps moving forward.

Step 01
i

Consult

We learn your space, your story, and what your guests should feel.

Step 02
ii

Curate

We handpick works from our growing roster of emerging Melbourne artists — made for your walls.

Step 03
iii

Rotate & Maintain

Every few months, new artists. Every visit, condition checked. Your space stays fresh, effortlessly.
