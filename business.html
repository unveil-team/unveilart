The Cycle

How it flows, step by step.

1
Install

UnveilArt delivers and professionally installs the artwork at your venue. Every piece comes with a QR tag and placement guidance.

2
Display

Guests discover the work. Each piece carries a QR tag linking to an instant-purchase page — no staff involvement needed.

3
Sell

A sale is made. Revenue splits automatically — the artist's share rises with the price, from 70% up to 80%, while UnveilArt and the venue split the rest equally. No invoices. No waiting.

4
Rotate

Sold out? A fresh collection arrives free of charge. New artists, new conversations. The walls stay alive. The cycle continues.
